📋 UI/UX Mockup Overview

  • Left (Step 1): Student Information — Collects name, email, student ID, course, year level, and program.
  • Center (Step 2): Personal Information — Collects birthday, birthplace, sex, status, religion, contact, and address.
  • Right (Step 3): Scholarship & Requirements — Selects scholarship, type, and uploads required documents.
  • Progress Indicator: Shows current step with visual progress bar. Completed steps show checkmarks.
  • Navigation: Back/Next buttons with final Submit button prominently displayed in green.
  • Layout: Single unified form container with consistent styling across all three steps.
